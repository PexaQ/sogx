## Initial Synthesis

- The closest immediately implementable runtime knobs are already in PlayCanvas: global `splatBudget`, LOD range clamps, coarse-first streaming, `minPixelSize`, `minContribution`, `alphaClipForward`, DPR, anti-aliasing, and renderer path detection.
- The most plausible novelty is an adaptive controller and benchmark methodology for low-end Android WebGL2/WebGPU, plus offline SOG variant generation that is tuned to fixed camera routes and device classes.
- The highest-risk but potentially interesting candidate is prefix/importance-ordered SOG. Matryoshka GS supports the idea at training time, but a heuristic postprocess must be tested carefully against SOG spatial-ordering and streamed chunk assumptions.
- Mobile-GS and WebSplatter make clear that sorting, alpha blending, overdraw, and memory pressure are the right bottlenecks to instrument, but their deeper renderer changes are beyond a small PlayCanvas application-layer prototype.
